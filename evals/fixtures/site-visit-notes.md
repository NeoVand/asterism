# Site visit notes: Nordberg quarry, winter logging pilot

## Context

The team visited the Nordberg limestone quarry on 6 October 2026 to plan a winter data-logging pilot on two L180 wheel loaders already fitted with the existing radar proximity warning system. The quarry runs two shifts, and loaders work in reverse about half of the time while loading haul trucks at the face.

## What we agreed

- Log raw radar returns, warning events, machine speed, gear and brake pressure on both loaders from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.
- Mount a reference lidar with RTK-GNSS on one loader to create independent ground truth for pedestrians and light vehicles.
- Record operator acknowledgements of warnings so nuisance alarms can be separated from genuine hazards.
- Survey the loading face and the main haul road once with a drone so the recordings can later be replayed in a virtual copy of the site.

## Risks we saw

Snow banks along the haul road produce frequent radar returns, and the site manager estimates operators currently ignore most reverse warnings near the stockpile. Cold starts below minus 20 °C delayed the data logger by up to 15 minutes in a previous trial. The quarry's light vehicles do not carry tags, so cooperative detection cannot be tested here.
