# Skeptic's review

1. Research Chapter 13 (`search_graph` with `chapters: [13]`): the ten strongest objections,
   the failure-mode table, and "evidence that would satisfy each reviewer". Also Chapter 12's
   reviewer checklist and Chapter 10's credibility criteria.
2. Match each objection to the user's plan or claim: does the plan answer it? With what evidence?
3. Render a `risks` block (objection → likelihood/impact → what closes it) or a `checklist` of
   reviewer questions, citing facts.
4. Close with the two or three changes that would most raise reviewer confidence. Reviewers are
   won by closing weaknesses, not by adding upside.
